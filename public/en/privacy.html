Privacy policy

How Nicola Capasso processes the personal data collected through the website and the contact form.

Last updated: October 2026

Data controller

Nicola Capasso, freelance photographer — info@nicolacapasso.photo. You can write to this address for any request regarding personal data.

Data collected

Through the contact form we collect: name, email, phone (optional), wedding date and location, message. Name, email and message are required; phone, wedding date and location are optional.

We do not track or profile you.

Legal basis

  • Form data is processed under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures / performance of a contract).

Purpose

Form data is used exclusively to reply to your enquiry and, if commissioned, for communications related to the photography service. Data is processed only by the providers necessary for the service — hosting and email, both supplied by Aruba S.p.A. (Italy), appointed as processor under Art. 28 GDPR — and is not disclosed.

Provision of data

Providing data is optional; failure to provide the necessary data prevents us from answering the enquiry or managing the assignment.

Automated processing

We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

Data processor (hosting)

The website is hosted on Aruba S.p.A. servers (Italy) and data is processed on servers in Italy.

Retention

Data collected through the contact form is deleted within 12 months.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict processing, data portability, and object. Write to info@nicolacapasso.photo. Requests are handled at no cost and within one month of receipt; the period may be extended by two further months for complex or numerous requests, informing you of the extension and the reasons within one month (Art. 12 GDPR).

You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it).
