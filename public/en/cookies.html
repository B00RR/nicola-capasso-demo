Cookies and other storage tools

What the site uses to work, why no cookie banner appears and how the Italian and English versions are served.

Last updated:

Data controller

Nicola Capasso, freelance photographer — info@nicolacapasso.photo. You can write to this address for any request regarding personal data.

What we use

On the public website we do not use cookies and we store no preference in your browser (no localStorage or sessionStorage). The Italian and English versions are served from separate server-side URLs (it/en): your language choice is not stored in your browser.

The WordPress admin area may set a temporary session cookie for authenticated users; this cookie is not used on the public site.

Why no cookie banner

No banner is shown because these technical tools do not require consent: under Art. 122 of the Italian Privacy Code and the Garante's guidelines of 10 June 2021, this information notice alone is sufficient for technical tools only. The site does not use profiling, analytics or advertising cookies.

Third-party cookies and services

We do not integrate Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or similar tracking tools.

External content

We do not embed external content (videos, maps, social media widgets). Some pages contain links to external profiles (Instagram, WhatsApp): these are simple links that open only if you select them, not automatic requests from the site.

Site resources (fonts, images, scripts, styles) are hosted on our own server: no automatic requests to external domains during browsing.

Managing

The site stores no cookies and no local preferences, so there is nothing to manage on our side. You can still limit or clear cookies and storage from your browser settings, for this and other websites.
